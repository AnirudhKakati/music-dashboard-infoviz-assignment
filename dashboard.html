Key Takeaways

Genre shift: Hip hop's share of top hits more than doubled from 18% in 1999 to 43% in 2019, while rock virtually disappeared from the charts.
Shorter & explicit: Songs shrank by 56 seconds on average, and explicit content nearly tripled — likely driven by hip hop's dominance and streaming-era incentives.
Star power: A small number of artists like Rihanna (25 hits) and Drake (23 hits) have consistently dominated, shaping the sound of two decades.
